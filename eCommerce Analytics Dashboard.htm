Business Question 2 Orders by Hour of Day — Promotion Timing
Recommendation: The highest order concentration occurs between 09:00–12:00 and 14:00–16:00. Run time-limited promotions at 09:00 (morning peak) and 14:00–15:00 (afternoon peak) to capture buyers already in an active purchasing session. Avoid promotions after 18:00 where order volume drops sharply.
